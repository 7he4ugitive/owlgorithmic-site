About

A small practice with a long-term view

Owlgorithmic is an independent quantitative research practice. We design, test, and operate systematic strategies for U.S. equity markets, combining careful data work with purpose-built software.

Every idea begins as a hypothesis and is evaluated against historical evidence before it is considered for use. We favour simple, explainable rules over complexity, and we treat risk management as part of the research rather than an afterthought.

Our work runs on the Owlgorithmic Platform, a private application for market-data preparation, strategy research, and portfolio backtesting. Access is by invitation, and authorized users sign in with their Google account. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Principles

How we work

01

Evidence first

02

03

Robust engineering

Research, backtesting, and operations share one auditable platform, so what is tested is what runs.
