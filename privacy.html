Summary. Owlgorithmic uses Google Sign-In only to confirm who you are when you access the Owlgorithmic Platform. We receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, files, or calendar, we do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Who we are

Owlgorithmic (“Owlgorithmic,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an independent quantitative research practice based in the United States. We operate the website at owlgorithmic.us and the Owlgorithmic Platform, a private research application for studying U.S. equity markets.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information in connection with:

  • the public website at owlgorithmic.us (the “Website”); and
  • the Owlgorithmic Platform, including its sign-in with Google (the “Platform”).

Together these are the “Services.” Access to the Platform is limited to individuals we have invited and authorized.

3. Information we collect

Information from Google Sign-In

When you sign in to the Platform with your Google account, Google shares the following with us, with your permission:

  • your name;
  • your email address and whether Google has verified it;
  • your profile picture, if you have one; and
  • a unique Google account identifier.

We request only the standard openid, email, and profile scopes. We never receive your Google password.

Information generated when you use the Platform

To keep the Platform secure and working, we record limited technical information, such as sign-in times, session identifiers, and the actions an authorized user takes in the application.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

Website visits

The Website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers, and it has no sign-up forms. The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are served by Google Fonts. Like most web services, these providers may automatically log technical data such as your IP address and browser type when your browser requests a page or font. Those logs are handled under the providers’ own privacy policies.

4. Google user data

Owlgorithmic’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, information received through Google Sign-In:

  • is used only to authenticate you, decide whether you are authorized to use the Platform, show your name and picture within the Platform, and contact you about your access;
  • is not sold, and is not used or transferred for advertising, retargeting, or interest-based advertising;
  • is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes;
  • is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models; and
  • is not read by humans unless you give us permission, it is needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, it is needed to comply with applicable law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

5. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • authenticate users and control access to the Platform;
  • operate, maintain, and secure the Services, including detecting and preventing unauthorized access;
  • respond to your messages and communicate with you about your access; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build marketing profiles.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose information only:

  • with service providers that host or operate the Services on our behalf, under obligations to protect it and to use it only to provide services to us;
  • for legal reasons, when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, regulation, or legal process, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Owlgorithmic, our users, or others;
  • in a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, in which case this policy will continue to apply to the information transferred; or
  • with your consent.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your Google account information for as long as you remain an authorized user of the Platform. Security and sign-in records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for security and troubleshooting. When your access ends, or when you ask us to delete your information, we delete or anonymize it within 30 days, unless we must keep it to comply with law or to resolve a dispute.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), access limited to authorized users, and credentials kept separate from application code. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Revoke Google access. You can remove Owlgorithmic’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page. You will then no longer be able to sign in to the Platform.
  • Access, correct, or delete. You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

10. California residents

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to request access to or deletion of it; to correct inaccurate information; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. As described above, we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than providing the Services. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

11. Children

The Services are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how we use Google user data, we will ask for your consent before applying it to data we have already collected.

13. Contact us

For questions or requests about this Privacy Policy or your information, email owlgorithmic6@gmail.com.
